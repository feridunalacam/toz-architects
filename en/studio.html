Sheet 01 · Studio

The office
is in Abbasağa.

Efe Bahri Toz leads the office, with Başak Toz and Macit Toz. The work is housing, interiors, existing buildings, and small civic work.

The slope, the party walls, and the ordinary day of Abbasağa are not a backdrop. Every job starts with the measure of the street.

Each job moves forward on paper: plan, section, material, light. What stays, what goes, what is added — the line stays thin until those three are decided. The four scales are set out on the practice page.

  1. 01

    See the place

    A photograph is not enough. The threshold, the slope, the neighbour’s window — taken by standing there.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Wall thickness, level, window height, where the stair lands. The drawing comes out of these numbers.

  3. 03

    Separate

    Kept, removed, repaired. The plan does not move until the three are written down.

  4. 04

    Draw

    Few decisions. A plan, a section, one elevation. The line thickens when the decision is clear.

  5. 05

    Choose the material

    The sample is judged on site, in that hour’s light — not under the office lamp.
