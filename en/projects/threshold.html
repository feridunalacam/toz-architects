Study 01

A house is not a list of rooms. First the threshold: where the door turns, the height the window sits at, the tread the hand meets. The plan is the result of these three.

Hall and rooms are as large as they need to be. Light enters from one face and stays on that face in the evening. The red line on the plan marks the threshold. The work starts there.
